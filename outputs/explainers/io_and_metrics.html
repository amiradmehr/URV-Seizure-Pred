What goes in, what comes out, and how it is scored

The three things you need to read any result from this pipeline, in math and in charts. Every chart is computed from 53,407 real validation decisions, so the numbers here are the numbers the model actually produced.

1 · Input

One example is 45 minutes of EEG ending at the decision instant, plus a three-value mask saying which electrodes physically existed. Nothing else: no patient ID, no time of day, no prior prediction.

the slice, for decision k in recording r nk = h + k·S·fs h = 691,200 samples, S = 60 s, fs = 256 Hz Xk = Z(r)[ : , nk−h : nk ] ∈ ℝ3×691200 reshaped into the encoder's unit of work U[m] = Xk[ : , 1280m : 1280(m+1) ] m = 0 … 539 plus the mask — exactly two of three electrodes exist in any recording a = (aBTE_L, aBTE_R, aCROSS) ∈ {0,1}³ , Σc ac = 2

2 · Output

One scalar per decision. It is not a seizure detection and it carries no time-to-onset: it answers only "will an onset begin in the next 10 minutes?"

the model emits a logit; the sigmoid maps it to a probability ℓ = wᵀ·LayerNorm([ ē ; a ]) + b w ∈ ℝ³⁵ p = σ(ℓ) = 1/(1 + e−ℓ) ∈ (0,1) read as p = P( ∃ onset τ ∈ (t , t + 600 s] | preceding 45 min ) a decision fires an alarm only after comparison with a threshold alarm = [ p ≥ θ ] θ is chosen afterwards, from the false-alarm budget

3 · Metrics that need no threshold

Both summarise the ranking over all thresholds at once, so neither depends on a choice of θ. They differ in what they treat as the baseline.

ROC AUC — probability a random positive outranks a random negative AUC = P( pi > pj | yi=1, yj=0 ) chance = 0.5 measured 0.5947 average precision — area under precision-recall AP = Σn (Rn − Rn−1)·Pn chance = prevalence = 0.00618 measured 0.0096 = 1.55× chance why AP is the honest one here at 0.62% prevalence a useless model still gets AUC 0.5 but AP 0.00618; AUC is inflated by the 53,077 negatives that are easy to rank low.

4 · Metrics that need a threshold

Everything clinical starts by fixing how many false alarms per hour are tolerable, then reading sensitivity off at that point. The threshold is an output of the budget, not a free parameter.

each negative decision stands for S seconds of interictal time interictal hours = N⁻ · S / 3600 = 53,077 · 60 / 3600 = 885 h FAR/h(θ) = FP(θ) / 885 at the 1 alarm/h budget used throughout this project θ = 0.4893 TP = 9 FP = 885 FN = 321 TN = 52,192 achieved FAR = 885 / 885 = 1.00 alarms/h decision level — every one-minute decision counts separately Sensdec = TP / (TP + FN) = 9 / 330 = 0.027

5 · The metric that actually matters

A patient needs one warning per seizure, not ten. Seizure-level sensitivity collapses each seizure's whole pre-onset window into a single verdict by taking the maximum — so nine misses and one hit still counts as caught.

Ks = the decisions targeting seizure s Senssz(θ) = |{ s : maxk∈Ks pk ≥ θ }| / |𝒮*| measured at 1 alarm/h 2/33 seizures = 0.061 why it differs so much from the decision-level number each seizure contributes SOP/S = 600/60 = 10 chances; one suffices.

6 · Calibration

AUC and AP only care about order. Brier asks whether p means what it says — whether decisions assigned p = 0.3 are followed by a seizure 30 % of the time.

mean squared error between probability and outcome Brier = (1/N) Σi (pi − yi)² measured 0.1116 the trap at low prevalence always predicting p = 0.00618 gives Brier ≈ 0.00614 while being useless, so Brier alone can look excellent for a model that never fires.

Reading these together

The four numbers answer different questions and can disagree. A model can improve AUC while losing seizures, which is exactly what the attention-pooling variant did: AUC rose to 0.6027 while seizure-level sensitivity fell to 1/33. Ranking improved in the bulk of the distribution and got worse at the high-confidence end where alarms are actually triggered.

Report order for this project: seizure-level sensitivity at a stated alarm budget first, AP second (with its chance ratio, never bare), AUC third, Brier only alongside a claim about probabilities. And always with the denominator — 33 seizures means a two-seizure change is noise.
